r410: Sky, light & weather as tiles
- Scenes: picture cards along the top (Clear day first); Save as mine and Reset all next to them.
- Time of day: one big day-to-night bar.
- Tiles for Sun, Clouds, Rain, Snow, Fog, Lightning, Wind, Night sky, Rainbow, Birds, Leaves, Lamps, Objects and Film, each saying what it is set to.
- Tap a tile to switch it on; tap again for its settings, which open under its row with Turn off, Reset and close.
- Objects tile holds fires, fireworks, trees, string lights and the list to remove them.
