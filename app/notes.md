r392: Dip search only finds dips in the ground
- Find the dips now reads the ground only: walls, sills, cars, railings and roofs are left out.
- Dips must be deeper than the scan's own noise; the panel shows the noise and the cut-off.
- Hollows over 250 mm deep (pits, steps, gaps in the scan) are left out and counted.
- Squares are sized to the scan's point density, so sparse drone scans no longer show speckle dips.
