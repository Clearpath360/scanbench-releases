r393: Dip search finds real dips beside cars and on noisy scans
- Gaps in the scan (under a parked car) no longer let the water out, so a dip next to a car is found.
- Ground is averaged over about 0.6 m before the search, so scan speckle is not taken for dips.
- A dip needs water at least the cut-off deep over 0.2 m² or more.
- The cut-off follows the scan's own noise (shown in the panel).
