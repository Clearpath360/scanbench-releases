# r417: settings for each placed object

- Pick a placed object (list or 3D) and its own settings open under the list (Sky, light & weather > Objects).
- Fires: size, flame height, light on the scan, sparks, smoke amount, smoke height, spread and colour.
- Smoke: amount, height, spread, puff size, colour. Fireworks: rockets a minute, height, burst size, colour.
- Sunbeam size and strength, pigeon count, tree height and lights, string light brightness, droop and spacing, confetti amount and timing.
- Same for all copies the settings to the rest of that kind; Reset puts the picked one back.
