# r427: rain on the block

- Ponding > picked dip > Rain on this block: rain falls on just that block in 3D, runs down, fills the dip and runs off the sides, with a time slider, Play/Pause, Stop and a live readout.
- Uses the same strength and length settings as the Floor levels rain test.
- Test dip (30 mm, 62 L): heavy rain fills it to 63 L and 30 mm after the rain; the rest runs off.
