# r431: see the water spread

- Rain on the block draws the water as it spreads: thin running water as light blue streams, puddles on the ground round the block, and water that overflowed the dip in orange so you can follow it downhill.
- Readout names the other dips the overflow reaches (real car park, dip 9 over an hour: reaches dips 1, 8 and 17).
- While it rains on the block, only the live water shows (the full-dip water from Find is hidden).
- Rain up to 3 hours, and up to 2 hours after, in both rain tests.
