- Rain falls on the ground round the block too (block only, 2x, 3x or 5x), so water from uphill runs in.
- Water runs downhill on its own surface at the speed the surface allows (Manning, diffusive wave), soaks in on soft ground, and goes round cars.
- Surface: tarmac/concrete, block paving, permeable paving, gravel or short grass (rough typical figures).
- Arrows show which way water runs; orange arrows and an "Overflowing here" marker show where the dip spills, in litres a minute.
- Summary: when it filled, litres spilled, ran in from round it, fell on it and soaked in.
