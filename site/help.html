30 October 2026
  • Zone, Section and Note now switch to the Plan when you pick them in 3D, so your clicks draw straight away.
  • The Walls, Roof, Floor and Delete buttons on a zone wrap onto two lines so none are cut off.
  • Drawings is now part of Model: Zones, Sections and Notes are under Mark up on the Model rail, and the top bar is Prep, Model, Compare.
  • Compare: Prep, Model, Drawings and the 3D and Plan buttons now take you out of Compare and straight there, instead of needing Done first.

  • Realsee models open in colour: a GLB whose pictures sit beside it as separate files (texture_0.jpg and so on) now uses them. Open the folder, the zip, or the GLB with its pictures; a GLB on its own asks for them.
  • A 3D model can also be opened as its unzipped folder (drop the folder on the window, or use XGRIDS LCC folder). A model split into several files is joined into one, and models with lots of big pictures no longer run out of memory.
  • Open a 3D model as a scan: GLB, GLTF or OBJ (with its MTL and pictures), or the ZIP it came in, such as a Realsee model download. Its surface is covered with small flat splats coloured from its pictures, so Measure, Plan, Sections and Clean up work on it. Read as metres; FBX is not read, so download the GLB instead.
