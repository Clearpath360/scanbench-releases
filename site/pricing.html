Ask a question

Questions

Do I need a card for the trial?

Yes. You add a card when you sign up, and nothing is taken until day 8. Cancel before then and you are not charged.

How do I cancel?

Open Help > Licence in the app and click Manage, renew or cancel, or use the link in your sign-up email. You can cancel any time.

What happens if my year runs out?

If the licence is not renewed, Scan Bench locks until you renew it. Your scans and files stay on your computer and are not touched.

Do my scans get uploaded anywhere?

No. Scan Bench runs on your computer and opens your files there. The only thing it sends is a licence check and, if you allow it, crash reports so we can fix problems.

What if it does not work for me?

Cancel during the trial and you pay nothing. After a payment, see the refund policy, or get in touch and we will try to fix it first.

Which scanners does it work with?

XGRIDS (PortalCam, Lixel) LCC and LCC2 folders open directly. Any Gaussian splat or point cloud in PLY, E57, SPZ, SPLAT or SOG works too, for example from Polycam, Luma, Postshot or KIRI.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. Scan Bench is Windows only.
