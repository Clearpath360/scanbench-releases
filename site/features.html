Features

Everything in Scan Bench

The main tools, grouped by job. The beta includes all of them.

Clean up

Select and delete in 3D with brush, box or lasso, fill behind removed cars and people, find holes, level, crop, set the scale, straighten. Save a cleaned PLY or a much smaller SPZ.

Measure

Lengths, areas and heights on the scan, with snapping to edges and walls. Heights read in millimetres by default.

Drawings

Plans, A-A and B-B sections and elevations with dimensions that never overlap. PDF sheets with title blocks, and DXF for CAD.

Compare

Line up scans of one site from different days: three matching spots, or automatic. Slider or flick between any two dates, fine tune with labelled nudges.

Timelapse

Fade, Wipe, Grow, Build up, Top down, Laser scan, Blocks, Sweep and Pop between dates. Still, slow move, orbit, keyframes, walk or fly path cameras. Rendered to MP4.

Walk-through films

Draw a route on the plan, aim each point, set heights in the side view. Smooth camera with no jerks at the points.

Volume and levels

Cut and fill between two dates, stockpiles against a base, cut and fill to a level, floor flatness and falls, ponding survey.

Sky and weather

Sun clock sky, clouds, rain with wet ground, snow, fog, lightning, fireworks, fire and smoke, northern lights and more.

Formats

XGRIDS LCC and LCC2 folders with level of detail, PLY (splat or point cloud), E57, SPZ, SPLAT, SOG.

VR

Meta Quest through Quest Link or Air Link from the same app.

On your computer

Everything runs on your machine. Nothing is uploaded, so big scans open as fast as your disk and graphics card allow.

Survey extras

Headroom map, CCTV coverage planner, roof pitch reader and spot levels.

What you need

Computer

Works on

  • Windows 10 or 11
  • A graphics card that can run WebGL2 (most from the last 8 years)
  • 16 GB memory recommended for big LCC scans

Best with

  • An Nvidia or AMD graphics card for the largest scans
  • A mouse with a wheel and a keyboard
  • An SSD for fast loading
Scan Bench

Desktop software for 3D scans: clean them up, measure them, compare them over time and turn them into films.
