1 of 6

Open and prepare

Get the scan ready before you work on it.

XGRIDS LCC and LCC2

Open the folder straight from the scanner, with level of detail, in real metres.

Any splat or point cloud

PLY, E57, SPZ, SPLAT, SOG and KSPLAT, from Polycam, Luma, Postshot, KIRI and more.

Level and square

The floor is levelled and the building turned square to the plan when the scan opens.

Straighten by hand

Drag a ring to tilt or turn the scan, or click 3 points that should be level.

Scale

Set the real size from a floor to ceiling height or a length you know.

Crop and work area

Keep only the part of the site you need.

Combine scans into one

Join floors, rooms, or inside and outside into one scan, lined up for you and blended at the joins.

2 of 6

Clean up

Take out what should not be there, and fill the gap.

Remove object

Click a car or a person to pick it, then Delete + fill rebuilds the ground behind it.

Trim box, lasso, brush and lines

Mark stray splats red and delete them, with undo.

Lonely splats

Finds floaters for you, with a strength slider.

Fix holes

Fill gaps from the surface beside them, find every hole, clone, repeat a bay, straighten a wall.

Patch area

Lock what must stay, sample clean surface, and fill a bigger gap with the shade matched.

Looks

Ghost, tint, outline or hide any object.

Save it smaller

Save a clean PLY, or an SPZ that is a fraction of the size.

3 of 6

Measure and survey

Numbers you can put in a report.

Lengths, areas and heights

Snap to walls, edges and corners in plan, 3D and sections. Millimetres by default.

Sections A-A and B-B

Live cross sections beside the plan, with spot heights, falls and a slice height map.

Volume

Stockpiles, cut and fill between two dates, and cut and fill to a level, with a PDF report and CSV.

Floor levels

Heights, flatness, falls and a spot grid for a car park, yard or floor.

Ponding

Where water would stand and how deep, with a dip survey PDF.

Site checks

Headroom map, CCTV coverage planner and roof pitch reader.

Quantities

Draw rooms and areas on the plan for floor, wall and paint quantities and a priced quote.

Drawing check

Lay the site drawing over the scan and see where it was built against it.

4 of 6

Compare over time

See how a site changes between visits.

Line up two visits

Automatically, or from a few matching points, with fine tune nudges.

Slide between dates

A slider or a flick between any two scans of the same site.

Cut and fill between dates

How much material came and went, as a colour map and numbers.

Timelapse

Fade, Wipe, Grow, Build up, Top down, Laser scan, Blocks, Sweep and Pop between dates, rendered to MP4.

5 of 6

Films and presenting

Show the scan to people who do not use 3D software.

Walk and fly routes

Draw a route on the plan, set heights in the side view, and get a smooth camera with no jerks.

Auto route from points of interest

Drop a few marks and a cinematic route is planned round them.

Sky, light and weather

Sun clock, clouds, rain with wet ground, snow, fog, lightning, fireworks and more, with one click scenes.

Render to MP4

16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5, ready for a client or social media.

Client viewer

One file your client opens in a browser to look round the scan.

VR

Step into the scan with a Meta Quest over Quest Link or Air Link.

6 of 6

Drawings and files

Take the work into CAD, BIM and the office.

Plans, sections and elevations

PDF sheets with title blocks, and dimensions that never overlap.

DXF

2D and 3D DXF on named layers for CAD.

Models

Trace walls, floors and roofs over the scan, then export OBJ, GLB with scan textures, DAE or IFC.

Schedules and reports

Room schedules, volume and level reports as PDF and CSV.

Everything in one ZIP

Drawings, models, schedules and pictures in one go.
